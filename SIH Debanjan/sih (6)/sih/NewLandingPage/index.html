How It Works

Simple steps to report and track issues

Upload Photo

Step 1

Capture and upload a photo of the issue you want to report. Our advanced image processing helps identify the type of problem automatically for faster routing to the right department.

Auto-Location Tagging

Step 2

Your location is automatically detected and tagged for precise issue mapping. GPS coordinates ensure municipal departments can quickly locate and address the reported problem.

Add Description

Step 3

Provide additional details with text or voice notes for better context. Voice-to-text conversion makes it easy to add descriptions on the go, ensuring complete information reaches the authorities.

Submit & Track

Step 4

Submit your report and track its progress until resolution. Real-time updates keep you informed about the status, assigned department, and expected resolution timeline.
